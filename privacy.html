Privacy policy for rclone (X Forge Labs off-site backup)

This app is a private tool. X Forge Labs uses it only to copy its own encrypted backups into its own Google Drive account. It reads and writes files only in that one account.

It collects no data from anyone else. Nothing is shared, sold or used for any other purpose. Google user data the app touches is the backup files themselves, which are encrypted before they leave the house.
